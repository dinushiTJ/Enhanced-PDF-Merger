About the tool

What is a PDF merger?

A PDF merger combines two or more PDF files into a single document. It can help when joining reports, invoices, assignments, applications, scanned documents, or supporting paperwork.

PDF Merger runs in modern web browsers and processes files on your device, so you can combine documents without sending them to an external service.

Useful for everyday paperwork

Merge documents for applications and records

Use PDF Merger to combine reports, invoices, financial records, employment letters, school documents, scanned records, visa paperwork, and other supporting documents into one file.

For important submissions, check the receiving office's file-size, page-order, naming, and document requirements before uploading your merged PDF.

Can I merge PDF files for free?

Yes. PDF Merger is free to use in your browser and does not require a subscription or software installation.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. The merger processes files locally in your browser. Your documents do not need to be uploaded to a server.

Can I combine multiple PDF files into one?

Yes. Select multiple PDF files, arrange their order, and merge them into one combined PDF document.

Does the PDF merger work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. It works in modern browsers on Mac, Windows, Linux, and supported mobile devices.
